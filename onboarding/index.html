WELCOME TO BROWSER RECAP

Your browser has a story.
Keep it yours.

Browser Recap turns your active browsing into daily, weekly, monthly, yearly and all-time recaps. Nothing is uploaded.

What gets tracked

The domain in your active tab and how long it stays active while Chrome is focused and your computer is not idle.

What stays off by default

Page titles. Full URLs are never stored. You can exclude any domain or pause tracking at any time.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Sessions are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. No account, analytics, ads, or external AI.

Old browser history

Optional later. You can import domain visit counts as historical clues, but Browser Recap never turns them into fake time spent.

You can review or erase everything from Settings at any time.
